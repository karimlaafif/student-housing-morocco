🌍 About the Project

Moving to another city to pursue higher education should be an exciting opportunity, not a financial struggle.

Every year, thousands of Moroccan students leave their hometowns to study in different cities. Many face the same challenges:

- Unaffordable accommodation: Rental prices can exceed what students can realistically afford.
- Lack of transparency: Students often have no reliable reference for comparing rental prices.
- Limited information: Finding a decent room in an unfamiliar city can be difficult.
- Unreliable intermediaries: Students may encounter misleading advertisements, unnecessary brokerage fees, or fraudulent listings.
- Finding roommates: Connecting with trustworthy students to share accommodation is not always easy.
- Geographical isolation: Students moving from smaller towns often lack local contacts and guidance.

We believe access to decent accommodation should not determine whether someone can pursue their education.

Student Housing Morocco aims to address these challenges through a collaborative, open-source platform built by students, for students.

🎯 Our Vision

To create a national student housing ecosystem where every student in Morocco can find affordable accommodation, access reliable housing information, and benefit from a supportive community.

🚀 Our Mission

To connect students, universities, accommodation providers, and local communities through accessible technology and collective action.

We aim to:

- Make student accommodation easier to discover.
- Promote transparent and fair rental information.
- Connect students looking for shared accommodation.
- Encourage student-to-student solidarity.
- Build trust through community verification and moderation.
- Collaborate with universities and student associations.
- Develop open-source tools that can benefit future generations.

💡 What Are We Building?

We envision more than a traditional accommodation marketplace.

We are building a Student Housing Network consisting of several interconnected components.

1. 🏠 Student Housing Marketplace

A dedicated space where students can discover:

- Individual rooms.
- Shared apartments.
- Studios.
- Student houses.
- Other affordable accommodation options.

Listings will provide relevant information, including rental prices, location, photographs, availability, and accommodation conditions.

2. 📊 Rental Price Transparency

Students should have access to meaningful rental information before making decisions.

We aim to develop a community-driven rental information system that allows students to:

- Report actual rental prices.
- Compare accommodation across neighbourhoods.
- Understand typical price ranges.
- Identify unusual price differences.
- Access anonymized rental market insights.

Our objective is not to impose rental prices but to provide transparent, contextual information.

3. 👥 Roommate Matching

Finding accommodation is easier when students can connect with others.

We aim to facilitate connections between students looking to:

- Share apartments.
- Find compatible roommates.
- Divide rental expenses.
- Form accommodation groups.

4. 🤝 Student-to-Student Solidarity

Students who have already lived in a city possess valuable knowledge.

We want to create a system where graduating students and former residents can help newcomers by sharing:

- Accommodation opportunities.
- Neighbourhood recommendations.
- Rental experiences.
- Practical city information.
- Advice about university life.

5. 🗺️ Student City Guide

Moving to a new city involves more than finding a room.

We aim to provide useful local information about:

- Neighbourhoods.
- Universities and campuses.
- Public transportation.
- Affordable restaurants and markets.
- Essential student services.
- Community-reported local guidance.

6. 🏛️ Institutional Collaboration

We believe sustainable student housing solutions require collaboration.

We aim to establish relationships with:

- Universities and higher education institutions.
- Student associations and university clubs.
- Local organizations and NGOs.
- Student representatives.
- Responsible accommodation providers.
- Public institutions and potential partners.

📍 Launch Strategy

National vision. Local implementation.

The project is designed for students across Morocco, with a gradual city-by-city rollout.

Our initial pilot will take place in:

Taroudant, Morocco 🇲🇦

Taroudant will serve as our initial testing ground to understand student needs, validate the concept, build our first community, and improve the platform before expanding.

Future expansion will depend on community participation, institutional collaboration, and the results of the pilot.

🛠️ Technology & Development

We aim to build an accessible, scalable, and maintainable technical infrastructure.

Our proposed technology stack:

Component| Proposed Technology
Frontend| Next.js + TypeScript
UI| Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui
Backend| Python + FastAPI
Database| PostgreSQL + PostGIS
Authentication| Supabase Auth or equivalent
File Storage| Supabase Storage / S3-compatible
Maps| OpenStreetMap + Leaflet
Deployment| Docker
Version Control| Git + GitHub

Important: This stack is a starting proposal. Technical decisions will be discussed openly with contributors before implementation.

We will initially favor a modular monolithic architecture to keep development accessible and maintenance manageable.

🔐 Trust, Safety & Privacy

Student safety is a fundamental requirement.

We aim to implement:

- Listing verification mechanisms.
- Suspicious listing reporting.
- Community moderation.
- Clear distinction between verified and unverified information.
- Responsible collection of personal data.
- Privacy-conscious student profiles.
- Measures to reduce fraudulent advertisements.

We will consider applicable Moroccan data protection requirements, including Law 09-08 and relevant CNDP guidance.

The platform will not guarantee the safety of a property or transaction simply because a listing appears on it.
